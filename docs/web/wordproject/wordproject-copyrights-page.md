# WordProject copyrights page

Recorded 2026-10-16. Facts come from the `url` above (fetched with `curl`)
and from the related disclaimer page
`https://www.wordproject.org/contact/new/disclaim.htm` (linked from the
footer of every Bible page).

## The site's licensing position (source: "Copyrights" section)

- The site thanks the translators and Bible Societies and claims their
  explicit or implicit authorization for the texts it hosts.
- It publishes the Bibles "in good faith claiming the right of fair use for
  non-profit and educational purposes and public benefit".
- It states that "Most, if not all, of the versions of the Bible in our
  site are in the Public Domain" and that most versions were translated many
  years ago and are no longer covered by copyright.
- It invites rights holders to produce evidence of a claim before texts are
  removed.
- The page asserts that the author of the Bible is God and that His rights
  are forever protected.

## Related disclaimer (source: disclaimer page, "Licensing Agreement" section)

- "The Bible content of this webpage that is in the Public Domain may be
  used in accordance with the Licensing agreement below".
- All audio on the site is copyrighted and may only be used for non-profit
  evangelization/education; linking to the audio servers without permission
  is prohibited. This repository uses only the text pages, not the audio.
- By using or distributing parts of the site, one accepts the site's terms
  (defect reporting, non-parody use, etc.).

## What this repository relies on

- For `en_US`, the KJV text at `wordproject.org/bibles/kj/` (a 17th/18th
  century English translation the site lists as public domain).
- For `ja_JP`, the Kougo-yaku text at `wordproject.org/bibles/jp/`.
- The crawlers attribute the source as `Wordproject` and its website
  `https://wordproject.org/`, matching the footer's organization statement.
